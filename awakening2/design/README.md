# Robot design round

## Three concepts (blockouts drawn by our look engine)
Sheets: `concept_A.jpg`, `concept_B.jpg`, `concept_C.jpg` (front, 3/4, side, back +
silhouettes at thumbnail size) and `concept_*_kneel.jpg`; all three in `concepts_overview.jpg`.

| | Concept | Silhouette at thumbnail | Character | Problems |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Hearth Warden: a hunched furnace giant, bell body, small domed head sunk between huge shoulder domes, barrel forearms, short massive legs, engine bells on the back | Mushroom/bell: reads instantly, nothing else in the film has it | Heavy, old, gentle; the furnace door is a natural home for the chest light | Head too small and plain, legs too long and thin, no detail |
| B | Lantern Samurai: brimmed helmet with a crest, masked face with eye slits, hanging shoulder plates, narrow waist, long legs | A thin humanoid with a hat: generic at thumbnail size | Dignified but stiff; the slit eyes cannot act | Reads as a standard mecha; not heavy; little room for moss |
| C | Diving-Bell Titan: round diving helmet with porthole eyes, boiler torso, long arms, short legs | A blob/bear | Cute, childlike | Porthole eyes = goggles (too close to the benchmark's goggle face); long-armed hunch drifts toward the famous Laputa robot |

## Decision: A, refined into "the Warden"
Why: it is the heaviest and most distinctive shape, it kneels into a hill-like mass that moss
and a tree can cover, its chest furnace carries the "faint light flickers on" beat, and its
small low head under a heavy brow gives us an expressive face (eyes + lids) without a human
mouth. Refinements for the final design:

1. A curved **shrine-roof carapace** on the back, eaves sweeping out over the shoulders with
   upturned corners (verdigris copper, like an old shrine roof). It makes the silhouette unique
   from every angle, gives soil, moss and a small old tree a believable place to grow, and its
   two halves swing open to reveal the engine bells before the launch.
2. A bigger head with a heavy V-shaped brow, two deep round lamp-eyes, and **eyelid shutters**
   that droop (dormant), crack open (waking) and level/narrow (resolute) — the eyes carry the
   emotion. A furnace-grille muzzle and lamp-housing "ears".
3. Shorter, heavier elephant legs; layered shoulder domes; barrel forearms with bands; big
   three-finger mitts; a skirt of tassets so the kneeling pose reads as a robed mass.
4. Palette: weathered vermilion lacquer (old torii red) over dark iron, verdigris roof, brass
   fittings, amber lamp eyes, warm furnace core — complementary to the forest greens, unlike
   the benchmark's grey-lilac golem.
