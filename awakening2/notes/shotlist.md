# Awakening II — draft shot list (v0, 2026-11-09 11:10)

Frame 1920×1040, 24 fps, ~75 s. Times in seconds. This list drives the backgrounds, effects,
sound cue sheet (`audio/cues.json`) and the animatic. It will be revised after the storyboard;
every consumer reads times from `audio/cues.json` / this file rather than hard-coding them.

World: an ancient forest of giant broadleaf trees (trunks 4–6 m thick, canopy 40–55 m up) in a
river valley at sunrise, mist on the floor. The robot is ~22 m tall standing, ~13 m kneeling
(on its right knee, left forearm resting on the left knee, head bowed). It has knelt so long
that moss, wildflowers, ferns and a small old tree (on its back/left shoulder) grow on it.
Sun: low, warm, from screen right/behind in most shots (backlight + rim), mist glows.
